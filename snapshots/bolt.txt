Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about the product and billing.
What are tokens?
Tokens are how Bolt measures the work it does on your project. Bolt uses tokens when it reads your prompts and project files, makes a plan, or writes code. Your plan includes a monthly token allowance, and you can check your balance on the
Billing & usage
page anytime.
For tips to help your tokens go further, see
Maximizing token efficiency
.
How do Teams plans work?
Teams plans give you centralized billing, team settings, and a shared workspace for collaborating on Bolt projects. You pay per team member, and paid members get a monthly token allowance based on your plan. Tokens aren’t shared between members. You can also add free members who can view team projects. But since free members don’t get tokens, they can’t send prompts.
Do tokens roll over from month to month?
On paid plans, yes. Unused tokens from your monthly allowance stay in your balance for up to two months, as long as your paid subscription is active. Free plan tokens don’t roll over. To see how token rollover works with examples, check the
Token rollover FAQs
.
What’s the difference between monthly and annual billing?
With an annual plan, you pay for the full year up front at a discounted price. With a monthly plan, you pay month by month. In both cases, your token allowance refreshes once a month, and any tokens you don’t use stay in your balance for up to two months after you receive them.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes. On the
Billing & usage
page, click
Change plan
under
Billing details
, and then pick the plan you want. Upgrades apply right away, and downgrades start at your next billing date. On an annual plan, that date is your annual renewal.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, anytime. On the
Billing & usage
page, click
Cancel subscription
under
Billing details
. Your plan and unused tokens stay active until your current billing period ends, then your account moves to the Free plan.
If you change your mind, you can
undo a cancellation
before it goes into effect.
What are the token limits on the Free plan?
The Free plan includes 1 million tokens a month, with a daily limit of 300,000. Free plan tokens don’t roll over.
